# JP Manna Studio — User Memories copy-paste (46 fields)

**Version:** 46-field design · May 2026 · 100 slots available  
**Change from prior pack:** Each concept gets its own clean field — no labeled subsections. Per-agent session continuity. Personal expanded to cover real life context.

For every field in Pickaxe Studio:
1. **Goal** = Memory Title (exact string below)
2. **Prompt** = the Prompt block (≤1024 chars each)
3. **Behavior** = **Collect, use, and display** for all 46

**Design reference (canvas):** `jp-manna-memory-design.canvas.tsx`  
**Index / matrix:** [`JP-MANNA-STUDIO-MEMORIES-SETUP.md`](JP-MANNA-STUDIO-MEMORIES-SETUP.md)

## GROUP 1 — Identity & UX (P0 — configure first)

---

### 1. Preferred Name & Location

**Memory Title:** `Preferred Name & Location`

**Prompt:**
```
Store preferred first name or nickname, city/state/country, time zone, and email if shared. Use name in every greeting. Use time zone for scheduling suggestions. Do not store PPV, comp tables, or contact lists here. Overwrite immediately when they correct you.
```

---

### 2. JP+ Rank

**Memory Title:** `JP+ Rank`

**Prompt:**
```
Store current Juice Plus+ partner rank: P, P+, QSC, SC, QSSC, SC2, QMD, MD, or PMD+. Tailor business coaching depth to rank — simpler how-tos at P/P+, deeper team and comp at SC+. Never store PPV amounts, comp math, or contact names here. Overwrite when rank changes.
```

---

### 3. Help Format Preferences

**Memory Title:** `Help Format Preferences`

**Prompt:**
```
Remember how this partner wants answers formatted across all pickaxes. Capture: steps vs big-picture strategy; scripts vs bullet outlines; tone (direct, warm, simple, brief). Apply on every agent — do not default to a style they've rejected. Overwrite when they update preferences.
```

---

### 4. Subscription & Suite Access

**Memory Title:** `Subscription & Suite Access`

**Prompt:**
```
Store which JP Manna tier this subscriber has: launch (Start here–focused) or full (six-tile suite). Optional: your exact Studio plan label. Start here: on launch tier, offer one gentle pointer to full suite — no invented pricing. Other agents: brief redirect to Start here if launch-only. Overwrite on upgrade.
```

## GROUP 2 — Brand Setup, Track A (P1)

---

### 5. Brand Mission & Why

**Memory Title:** `Brand Mission & Why`

**Prompt:**
```
Store this partner's brand origin story and mission in 2–4 sentences: why they joined JP+, what they believe, what they want to create. Marketing and Manna read before any audience-facing copy. Overwrite after Brand setup redo or explicit brand change.
```

---

### 6. Ideal Audience

**Memory Title:** `Ideal Audience`

**Prompt:**
```
Store the partner's ideal customer avatar: name or type, main struggles, desires, and what they are searching for. Marketing uses to frame posts. Manna uses for DMO targeting. Keep to 3–5 short lines. Overwrite when Brand setup is redone or avatar changes.
```

---

### 7. Brand Differentiation

**Memory Title:** `Brand Differentiation`

**Prompt:**
```
Store what makes this partner's approach unique among JP+ partners: their angle, mantra, and 1–2 things they do differently. Marketing filters ideas through this. Overwrite after Brand setup redo. Keep to 2–3 sentences.
```

---

### 8. Voice & Tone Settings

**Memory Title:** `Voice & Tone Settings`

**Prompt:**
```
Store how this partner writes: energy/tone (warm, bold, calm, etc.), typical post length (short/medium/story), emoji use (none/low/high), invite style (soft/balanced/direct), 2–3 signature phrases, and 1–2 sample sentences in their voice. All content drafts match these settings. Overwrite on Brand setup refresh or style update.
```

---

### 9. Content Pillars

**Memory Title:** `Content Pillars`

**Prompt:**
```
Store 3–5 content pillars (topic themes), main platforms (FB, IG, email, etc.), and preferred content formats (Reels, carousels, stories, long-form). Marketing suggests only pillar-fit ideas. Overwrite when pillars or platforms change.
```

---

### 10. Content Guardrails

**Memory Title:** `Content Guardrails`

**Prompt:**
```
Store topics to avoid, words to never use, and compliance boundaries for this partner's content. Always include: no disease/cure/prevent claims; no weight-loss product claims; no income projections (use IDS for earnings); no bad before/after promises. Allowed when framed correctly: helped/improved stories, nutritional support, supplemental income, time flexibility. See jp-compliance-guidelines.md. Add partner-specific guardrails. Marketing and Manna enforce on every draft. Overwrite when boundaries change.
```

---

### 11. Brand Setup Stage

**Memory Title:** `Brand Setup Stage`

**Prompt:**
```
Track Brand setup (StartPoint) progress for resume. Store current stage: 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, or complete. Optional: last step finished. Start here checks this at Brand setup open — if complete, do not restart unless asked. Update each stage completion or pause.
```

---

### 12. Brand Setup Complete

**Memory Title:** `Brand Setup Complete`

**Prompt:**
```
Record whether Brand setup (voice profile) is finished: yes or no, plus date completed if known. When yes, all agents assume voice, audience, and pillar memories are active. When no, Marketing does not assume full voice exists. Set yes at Stage 5. Reset only if they redo Brand setup.
```

## GROUP 4 — Business Operations (P0/P1)

---

### 21. DMO Goals Current

**Memory Title:** `DMO Goals Current`

**Prompt:**
```
Store this week or this month's DMO focus for Manna: 2–3 bullets covering outreach targets, follow-up queue, Power Hour theme, and team touches. Manna reads before Power Hour planning and daily pull. Not a CRM dump — topic-level only. Refresh weekly or when focus changes. Overwrite old bullets.
```

---

### 22. Business Goals

**Memory Title:** `Business Goals`

**Prompt:**
```
Store this partner's JP+ business goals in 2–4 bullets: rank target, customer count, team building, consistency milestones. No dollar amounts, income promises, or timelines. Manna and Lori tailor coaching to these. Update when goals shift. Move completed wins to Manna Session Notes.
```

---

### 23. Business Challenges

**Memory Title:** `Business Challenges`

**Prompt:**
```
Store 1–3 current business blocks in the partner's own words: time constraints, fear of posting, follow-up avoidance, imposter feelings, etc. Manna and Lori use these to tailor support. No diagnosing. Update when blocks change or resolve. Keep short — topic labels, not long explanations.
```

---

### 24. IDS Compliance Acknowledged

**Memory Title:** `IDS Compliance Acknowledged`

**Prompt:**
```
Record whether this partner has been shown IDS / results-vary framing: yes or no, plus date if known. All business agents use results-vary language on income topics when yes. Never make specific income promises, timelines, or replace-income claims regardless of this setting. Set yes on first compliant income discussion.
```

---

### 25. Power Hour Schedule

**Memory Title:** `Power Hour Schedule`

**Prompt:**
```
Store how this partner runs Power Hour: best day and time, typical length (e.g. 50 min), and primary focus (outreach, follow-up, admin, or mixed). Manna uses to plan the daily pull and suggest timing. Overwrite when schedule or focus changes.
```

---

### 26. Follow-Up Rhythm

**Memory Title:** `Follow-Up Rhythm`

**Prompt:**
```
Store this partner's follow-up habit: Hot/Warm/Cold cadence, 24-7-30 preference, weekly batch style, or other rhythm. Contacts uses for who-is-due framing. Manna uses for DMO planning. Never store prospect names here. Overwrite when rhythm changes.
```

---

### 27. Outreach Style

**Memory Title:** `Outreach Style`

**Prompt:**
```
Store preferred outreach approach: curiosity opener vs direct ask; channel preference (text, voice note, phone call, DM). Manna and Contacts use for scripting. Marketing uses for DM tone. Overwrite when style changes.
```

## GROUP 8 — Personal & Faith (P2)

---

### 34. Personal Life Context

**Memory Title:** `Personal Life Context`

**Prompt:**
```
Store recurring life topics and active personal projects in the partner's own words — what they come back to in Personal. Examples: lawn and garden care, making dog food, homeschooling plans, home renovation, local community involvement, cooking experiments. Update as new recurring topics emerge. Rotate out topics they haven't mentioned in a long time. Max 6 bullets.
```

---

### 35. Household & Family Context

**Memory Title:** `Household & Family Context`

**Prompt:**
```
Store foundational household context that makes Personal answers more relevant: family structure (married, single, kids), children's ages or school grades, pets and their names/breeds, home type (rural, suburban, apartment). Personal uses to give contextually accurate advice (e.g. yard size matters for lawn questions, dog breed matters for nutrition questions). Overwrite when family situation changes.
```

---

### 36. Learning & Interests

**Memory Title:** `Learning & Interests`

**Prompt:**
```
Store hobbies, active learning pursuits, and personal interests: what they're currently reading, studying, building, or practicing. Examples: watercolor painting, guitar, sourdough baking, woodworking, learning Spanish, following a sports team. Personal uses to personalize recommendations and continue ongoing conversations. Update as interests change or new ones emerge.
```

---

### 37. Personal Off Topics

**Memory Title:** `Personal Off Topics`

**Prompt:**
```
Store topics this partner does not want raised in Personal: e.g. comp plan details, heavy marketing drafts, political debates, medical diagnosis requests, topics they want to leave at the door. Faith and scripture are in scope unless listed here. When an off-topic comes up, give a brief answer or route to the right tile (Manna, Marketing, Health). Keep to 1–5 short topic labels. Overwrite when boundaries change.
```

---

Personal uses this on every scripture reference or verse request — ask once on first faith turn if unknown, then never ask again. Default NIV if not set. Business agents do not lead with scripture unless the partner bridges the topic. Overwrite on new preference.

### 39. Faith Study Focus
